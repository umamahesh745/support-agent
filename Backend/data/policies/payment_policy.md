# Payment Policy

This policy explains the payment methods accepted by ShopEasy.

## Accepted Payment Methods
ShopEasy accepts UPI, debit cards, credit cards, net banking, ShopEasy Wallet, and Cash on Delivery.

## Cash on Delivery
Cash on Delivery is available for orders up to 10,000 rupees. A COD fee of 49 rupees is charged per order. You can pay the delivery agent by cash or by scanning a UPI QR code.

## EMI Options
No-cost EMI is available on credit cards from selected banks for orders of 3,000 rupees or more. EMI options are shown on the payment page.

## Payment Failures
If money is deducted but the order is not confirmed, do not pay again. The amount is automatically refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. If it is not refunded, contact ShopEasy support with the transaction reference number.

## Payment Security
All payments are processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. ShopEasy never asks for your card PIN, CVV, OTP, or UPI PIN over phone, chat, or email. If someone asks for these details claiming to be from ShopEasy, do not share them and report it to support.

## Invoices
A GST invoice is emailed after delivery. You can also download it from My Orders.
