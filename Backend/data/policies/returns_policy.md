# Return Policy

ShopEasy wants you to be happy with every purchase. This policy explains when and how you can return a product.

## Return Window
You can request a return within 7 days of delivery. The 7 days are counted from the delivery date shown on your order page. Return requests raised after 7 days will not be accepted, except for warranty claims (see the Warranty Policy).

## Eligible Items
Most products are eligible for return if they are unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories, manuals, and freebies. Eligible products include Wireless Earbuds, Smartwatch, Bluetooth Speaker, Power Bank, USB-C Charger, Mechanical Keyboard, and Laptop Bag.

## Non-Returnable Items
Phone Cases cannot be returned once the seal is opened, for hygiene reasons. Products that are physically damaged by the customer, missing the serial number, or used beyond basic testing are also not eligible for return.

## Damaged or Wrong Product
If you receive a damaged, defective, or wrong product, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the product and packaging. ShopEasy will arrange a free pickup and send a replacement, or issue a full refund if a replacement is not available.

## How to Request a Return
Go to My Orders, select the order, and click Return. Choose a reason and upload photos if the product is damaged. A pickup will be scheduled within 2 business days. Keep the product packed and ready for pickup.

## Quality Check
After pickup, the product goes through a quality check at our warehouse within 2 business days. If the product passes the check, the refund is initiated. If it fails, the product is sent back to you and the return request is closed.
