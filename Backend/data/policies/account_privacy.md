# Account and Privacy Policy

This policy explains how to manage your ShopEasy account and how we protect your data.

## Creating an Account
You can create an account using your mobile number or email address. A one-time password (OTP) is sent to verify your number.

## Updating Account Details
You can update your name, email, phone number, and saved addresses from My Account. Changing the phone number requires OTP verification on both the old and the new number.

## Forgot Password
Click Forgot Password on the login page and enter your registered email or phone number. A reset link or OTP will be sent to you.

## Deleting Your Account
You can request account deletion from My Account under Privacy Settings. Accounts with pending orders, returns, or refunds cannot be deleted until they are completed. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

## How We Use Your Data
ShopEasy uses your personal details only to process orders, deliver products, provide customer support, and send order updates. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## Verification in Customer Support
To protect your account, the support team may ask you to confirm your registered email or phone number before sharing order or refund details.
