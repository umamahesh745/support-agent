# Warranty Policy

This policy explains the warranty on products sold by ShopEasy.

## Warranty Period by Product
Wireless Earbuds, Smartwatch, Bluetooth Speaker, Power Bank, and Mechanical Keyboard have a 1 year manufacturer warranty. USB-C Charger has a 6 month warranty. Laptop Bag and Phone Case are covered only for manufacturing defects reported within 30 days of delivery.

## What the Warranty Covers
The warranty covers manufacturing defects such as the product not powering on, charging failures, battery defects, faulty buttons, and connectivity problems that are not caused by the customer.

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover
The warranty does not cover physical damage, water damage, damage caused by using unofficial chargers, normal wear and tear, or products that were opened or repaired by an unauthorized person.

## How to Claim Warranty
Contact ShopEasy support with your order ID, a short description of the problem, and a photo or video of the issue. Our team will verify the claim and arrange a free pickup. The product will be repaired or replaced within 7 to 10 business days after it reaches the service center.

## Proof of Purchase
The order ID and GST invoice from ShopEasy are valid proof of purchase for warranty claims.
