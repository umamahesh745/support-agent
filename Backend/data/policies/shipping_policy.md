# Shipping Policy

This policy explains delivery charges, timelines, and order tracking.

## Delivery Charges
Delivery is free for orders of 999 rupees or more. Orders below 999 rupees have a delivery charge of 49 rupees.

## Delivery Timelines
Orders are usually shipped within 1 business day. Delivery takes 2 to 4 business days in metro cities such as Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, and Kolkata. Other locations take 4 to 7 business days. Remote areas may take up to 10 business days.

## Order Status Meaning
Placed means your order is confirmed and is being packed. Shipped means your order has left our warehouse and is with the courier partner. Delivered means the order has been delivered to your address. Cancelled means the order was cancelled and will not be delivered.

## Tracking Your Order
Once your order is shipped, you will receive an SMS and email with the tracking link. You can also track your order anytime from My Orders using your order ID.

## Delayed Delivery
If your order has not been delivered 2 days after the expected delivery date, contact ShopEasy support with your order ID. We will check with the courier partner and update you within 24 hours.

## Failed Delivery Attempts
The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. If all attempts fail, the order is returned to our warehouse and cancelled. Prepaid orders are then refunded in full.

## Changing the Delivery Address
The delivery address can be changed only while the order status is Placed. Once the order is Shipped, the address cannot be changed.
