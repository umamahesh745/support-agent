# Refund Policy

This policy explains how refunds work for cancelled orders and returned products.

## When Refunds Are Initiated
A refund is initiated when a prepaid order is cancelled before shipping, or when a returned product passes the quality check. Cash on Delivery orders cancelled before delivery do not need a refund because no payment was made.

## Refund Status Meaning
Every refund has one of three statuses. Requested means the refund request is received and is under review. Approved means the refund is approved and the payment is being sent to you. Processed means the money has been sent from ShopEasy's side and should reach your account within the bank timeline.

## Refund Timelines
After a refund is processed, UPI refunds reach your account within 1 to 3 business days. Debit and credit card refunds take 5 to 7 business days. Net banking refunds take 3 to 5 business days. ShopEasy Wallet refunds are instant.

## Refunds for Cash on Delivery Orders
For returned Cash on Delivery orders, the refund is sent to your bank account or UPI ID. You will be asked to add these details when you request the return. Bank refunds take 5 to 7 business days after processing.

## Refund Amount
You receive the full product price for eligible returns. The COD fee and shipping charges are not refunded, except when the product was damaged, defective, or wrong.

## Delayed Refunds
If your refund is marked Processed but you have not received it after the timeline above, please check with your bank first using the refund reference number. If the issue continues, contact ShopEasy support and we will raise a ticket with our payments team.
