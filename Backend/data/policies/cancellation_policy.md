# Cancellation Policy

This policy explains when and how you can cancel an order.

## When You Can Cancel
You can cancel an order free of charge while its status is Placed. Once the order is Shipped, it cannot be cancelled. In that case, you can refuse the delivery or request a return after delivery.

## How to Cancel
Go to My Orders, select the order, and click Cancel Order. Choose a reason and confirm. You will receive a cancellation confirmation by SMS and email.

## Refund After Cancellation
For prepaid orders, a refund is initiated automatically after cancellation, and the status starts as Requested. Please see the Refund Policy for timelines. Cash on Delivery orders do not need a refund.

## Cancellation by ShopEasy
ShopEasy may cancel an order if the product is out of stock, the address is not serviceable, or the payment could not be verified. In such cases you will be informed by SMS and email, and prepaid orders will be refunded in full.

## Refusing a Delivery
If you refuse a shipped order at the time of delivery, it is returned to our warehouse. Prepaid orders are refunded after the product reaches the warehouse, which usually takes 5 to 7 business days.
