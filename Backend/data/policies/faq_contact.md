# Frequently Asked Questions and Contact

## Customer Support Hours
ShopEasy chat support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Phone support is available from 9 AM to 9 PM, Monday to Saturday.

## Contacting a Human Agent
If the chat assistant cannot solve your problem, you can ask to talk to a human agent at any time. A support ticket will be created and a human agent will contact you within 24 hours. High priority issues such as payment problems are handled within 4 hours.

## Where Is My Order
You can check the current status of your order from My Orders or by sharing your order ID with the chat assistant.

## Can I Exchange a Product
ShopEasy does not offer direct exchanges. You can return the product within 7 days of delivery and place a new order for the item you want.

## Do You Deliver Internationally
No. ShopEasy currently delivers only within India.

## Are Products Genuine
Yes. All products sold on ShopEasy are 100 percent genuine and sourced directly from brands or authorized distributors.

## How Do I Give Feedback
You can rate your order experience from My Orders after delivery, or rate the chat conversation at the end of the chat.
